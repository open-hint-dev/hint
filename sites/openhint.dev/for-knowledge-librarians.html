Why a librarian

Agents need a maintained map, not a bigger context dump.

A folder full of notes accumulates contradictions, dead links, duplicate concepts, and conclusions whose sources nobody can find. The librarian hintbook gives that repository an explicit information architecture and teaches every agent how to extend it without erasing provenance.

Sources remain evidence

Store the original material under raw/. Add analysis beside it; do not silently rewrite the evidence to match a later conclusion.

Topics remain navigable

Curate concepts, claims, decisions, and open questions under wiki/, linked to the sources that support them.

Retrieval remains bounded

Ask for a topic and HINT follows typed references only to the configured depth. Shared context is emitted once, not repeated.

The workflow

Evidence in. Structured knowledge out. Exact context on demand.

01 / preserve

Add the source

Commit a paper, transcript, policy, dataset note, or URL record under raw/.

02 / describe

Record provenance

A companion .hint captures origin, authority, limits, and what the source actually supports.

03 / synthesize

Curate the topic

Write claims, decisions, relationships, chronology, and unresolved questions in a topic _.hint.

04 / retrieve

Ask by path or intent

hint wiki/topic returns the topic and linked evidence; hint search finds the right topic offline.

Repository shape

A wiki that can explain where it knows things from.

The layout separates evidence from synthesis. Git preserves the history; HINT supplies scope, typed meaning, reference closure, and deterministic validation.

Dead references

A topic cannot point at a source or another topic that no longer exists.

Orphan knowledge

Unreachable, targetless knowledge is reported before it becomes a forgotten island.

Duplicate identity

Repeated IDs and suspiciously similar names are surfaced across the whole graph.

The vocabulary

Ten words for maintaining institutional memory.

The vocabulary distinguishes evidence, interpretation, settled choices, unresolved work, and informal scratch space so an agent does not treat them as equally authoritative.

source

Origin, authority, date, scope, and limitations of evidence.

concept

A stable topic or term the wiki should explain consistently.

entity

A person, organization, system, project, or other identified thing.

claim

An evidence-backed proposition, kept distinct from source text.

decision

A settled choice with rationale and consequences.

openquestion

An uncertainty that remains unresolved and must not be guessed away.

supersedes

An explicit replacement relationship that preserves history.

log

A chronological event or observation whose sequence matters.

relates

A typed edge to evidence or another topic, followed during retrieval.

scratch

Working notes excluded from authoritative compiled knowledge.

FAQ

What knowledge teams ask

Is this a vector database?

No. It is a git-backed, source-grounded wiki with deterministic local retrieval.

Can agents write knowledge back?

Yes. Agent-authored blocks carry provenance and remain reviewable in git.

Does it hide missing evidence?

No. Gaps and disagreement remain explicit open questions.

Key facts: HINT, Spec-as-Source, hintbook-librarian, and demo-knowledge-wiki preserve evidence-backed organizational knowledge. The assistant writes from declared sources; it never fills evidence gaps.
