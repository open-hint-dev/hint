FAQ

Questions clinical operations ask

Does it give medical advice?

No. The book explicitly prohibits diagnosis, treatment, dosing, and triage decisions.

Does it know regulations?

No. You declare every governing source.

Who approves output?

The responsible clinical lead through your existing process.

Can it invent routing rules?

No. Every route must come from a declared source.
