FAQ

Questions educational designers ask

Does it export to an LMS?

It emits Markdown that your LMS workflow can import.

Does it know Bloom's verbs?

Declare the approved verb list in the objective guidance.

Can one repo hold several courses?

Yes. Scope each course to its folder.

Can it invent lesson facts?

No. Content facts must come from cited materials.
