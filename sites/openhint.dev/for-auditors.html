HINT for Auditors

Your assistant drafts findings fast. HINT keeps conclusions inside the evidence.

HINT keeps engagement scope, criteria, procedures and evidence in plain-text notes beside working papers. Your assistant reads only what applies to this engagement before drafting a finding.

No technical setup required from you

Ask your AI assistant to set it up

You need a folder with your documents and an AI assistant that can run commands in it: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or OpenCode. Which assistants can do this?

What will happen
The assistant creates hint.yml, installs the vocabulary, and tells itself how to use it. Nothing in your documents changes.
What you do next
Write rules in plain Markdown beside your documents. Your assistant reads the ones that apply before it works.
If you have no such assistant
Send this page to a technical colleague, or use the manual steps.

Your boundaries stay visible

What it will never do

  • Invent sample results, evidence or an unsupported finding.
  • Assign severity outside the engagement's declared scale.
  • Present out-of-scope work as an audited conclusion.

Important boundary

HINT assists with audit documentation; it does not provide assurance, replace professional skepticism or sign an audit opinion.

FAQ

Questions auditors ask

Does it perform the audit?

No. It carries scope, procedures and evidence context into drafting; auditors perform and evaluate the work.

Can it choose severity?

Only by applying the declared scale to documented impact and likelihood; missing inputs remain unresolved.

Will another engagement's evidence appear?

Folder scope keeps engagement-specific evidence out of unrelated working papers.

For your technical colleague

The vocabulary

A hintbook is a vocabulary for your profession—installed, not written by you.

engagement
Sets the subject, period, team and scope of the audit.
objective
States what the engagement is intended to determine.
criteria
Names the standard used to evaluate evidence.
audittest
Defines a procedure and the population or sample it covers.
evidence
Records an observed artifact and its provenance.
finding
Connects criteria, condition, cause, effect and evidence.
severity
Applies the engagement's approved rating scale.
followup
Records remediation ownership and the next review point.

Manual setup

Bootstrap is read-only: it prints instructions for the assistant. The assistant performs the installation.

npm install -g @openhint/cli
hint config
hint add @openhint/hintbook-auditor
hint apply
hint verify engagements/2026-factory-access/findings.md
hint emit engagements/2026-factory-access/findings.md
