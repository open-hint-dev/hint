FAQ

Questions auditors ask

Does HINT perform the audit?

No. It governs workpapers and findings from evidence you collected.

Can it read a compliance repository?

Yes, through read references or a vendored control source.

How is independence handled?

Declare the constraints in the engagement scope.

Can it invent findings?

No. Every finding must cite evidence ids.
