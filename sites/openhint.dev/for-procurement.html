HINT for Procurement

Your assistant drafts RFPs fast. HINT keeps them fair and source-based.

HINT keeps requirements, timelines, evaluation rules and approvals in plain-text notes beside tender documents. Your assistant reads the rules for this procurement before drafting supplier-facing language.

The package is named for the role: @openhint/hintbook-procurement-specialist.

No technical setup required from you

Ask your AI assistant to set it up

You need a folder with your documents and an AI assistant that can run commands in it: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or OpenCode. Which assistants can do this?

What will happen
The assistant creates hint.yml, installs the vocabulary, and tells itself how to use it. Nothing in your documents changes.
What you do next
Write rules in plain Markdown beside your documents. Your assistant reads the ones that apply before it works.
If you have no such assistant
Send this page to a technical colleague, or use the manual steps.

Your boundaries stay visible

What it will never do

  • Invent a budget, deadline, approval or vendor fact.
  • Write a brand-specific requirement without a justified source.
  • Expose confidential evaluation weights in the RFP.

Important boundary

HINT assists with procurement documentation; your policies, legal review and delegated authority govern the actual process and award.

FAQ

Questions procurement specialists ask

Does it select a supplier?

No. It keeps criteria and evidence structured; authorized evaluators make and approve the decision.

How is vendor neutrality enforced?

Declare outcome-based requirements and prohibit unsupported brand references; reviewers still assess legitimate exceptions.

Do weights have to total 100?

Only if your declared evaluation policy says so. HINT does not invent a scoring rule.

For your technical colleague

The vocabulary

A hintbook is a vocabulary for your profession—installed, not written by you.

tender
Sets the procurement, authority and document boundary.
need
States the business outcome behind the purchase.
budget
Records an authorized budget from a named source.
requirement
States a supplier-facing need with a stable identifier.
exclusion
Marks an internal rule that must not enter the RFP.
question
Records an approved supplier clarification.
criterion
Names a factor evaluators apply consistently.
weight
Records confidential scoring importance where authorized.

Manual setup

Bootstrap is read-only: it prints instructions for the assistant. The assistant performs the installation.

npm install -g @openhint/cli
hint config
hint add @openhint/hintbook-procurement-specialist
hint apply
hint verify tenders/monorail/evaluation.md
hint emit tenders/monorail/evaluation.md
