FAQ

Questions procurement ask

Does it fit public procurement?

It governs documents; your statutory procedure still controls.

Can bidders see the weights?

Only if you deliberately publish an internal artifact; public emit omits them.

Does it check scoring math?

The declared checklist requires weights to total 100.

Can it favor a vendor?

No. Vendor-neutral requirements are binding.
