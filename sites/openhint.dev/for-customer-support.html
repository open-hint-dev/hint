Does it integrate with a helpdesk?

It governs content; your helpdesk remains the delivery tool.

Can a live-chat agent use it?

Yes, if it can run HINT or connect through MCP.

Does it support several languages?

Write locale-specific policies under separate scopes.

Can it invent an ETA?

No. Dates must come from a sourced known issue.
