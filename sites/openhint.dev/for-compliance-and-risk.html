FAQ

Questions compliance & risk ask

Is HINT a GRC tool?

No. It governs the documents a GRC system stores.

Does it know regulations?

No. You declare and cite the applicable instruments.

Can one repo cover several frameworks?

Yes. Scope them per unit.

Does it approve exceptions?

No. Approval remains with the responsible owner.
