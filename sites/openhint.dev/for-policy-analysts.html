HINT for Policy Analysts

Your assistant drafts briefs fast. HINT keeps evidence separate from judgment.

HINT keeps the issue, cited instruments, options, criteria and impacts in plain-text notes beside policy documents. Your assistant reads the declared frame before comparing options or writing a recommendation.

No technical setup required from you

Ask your AI assistant to set it up

You need a folder with your documents and an AI assistant that can run commands in it: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or OpenCode. Which assistants can do this?

What will happen
The assistant creates hint.yml, installs the vocabulary, and tells itself how to use it. Nothing in your documents changes.
What you do next
Write rules in plain Markdown beside your documents. Your assistant reads the ones that apply before it works.
If you have no such assistant
Send this page to a technical colleague, or use the manual steps.

Where it helps

Three situations your team will recognize

Before: A brief cites a statute and section number from memory.

Every instrument must be declared

Missing authority becomes a research gap instead of a precise-looking citation.

Before: An impact figure appears without evidence.

Numbers retain their sources

Fiscal and social estimates remain linked to the evidence and assumptions that produced them.

Before: Options are framed so one conclusion is inevitable.

The same criteria apply to every option

Trade-offs stay visible and recommendations explain where judgment begins.

Your boundaries stay visible

What it will never do

  • Cite an unverified instrument or provision.
  • State an impact figure without a source.
  • Invent a stakeholder position or use partisan framing.

Important boundary

HINT supports policy analysis, not legal advice or democratic authority. Applicable law, consultation and accountable decision-making remain external.

FAQ

Questions policy analysts ask

Which jurisdictions does it know?

None by default. You declare the instruments and sources that govern the policy area.

Does it provide legal advice?

No. It structures cited policy context and identifies gaps for qualified review.

How is neutrality enforced?

Declare a neutral style, common evaluation criteria and prohibited framing; reviewers still judge whether the analysis is balanced.

For your technical colleague

The vocabulary

A hintbook is a vocabulary for your profession—installed, not written by you.

issue
Defines the policy question and its boundary.
mandate
Names who requested the analysis and for what decision.
regulation
Identifies a cited legal or regulatory instrument.
provision
Records the exact cited section that applies.
option
Defines one alternative with a stable identifier.
impact
Records an effect, dimension and supporting source.
tradeoff
Separates competing effects without hiding judgment.
recommendation
States a conclusion together with its justification.

Manual setup

Bootstrap is read-only: it prints instructions for the assistant. The assistant performs the installation.

npm install -g @openhint/cli
hint config
hint add @openhint/hintbook-policy-analyst
hint apply
hint verify issues/lot-48-park/options.md
hint emit issues/lot-48-park/options.md
