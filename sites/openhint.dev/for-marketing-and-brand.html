HINT for Marketing & Brand

Your assistant writes copy fast. HINT keeps it on-brand and supportable.

HINT keeps voice, approved terms, channel limits and proof rules in plain-text notes beside each brand's work. Your assistant reads the rules for this client and campaign before drafting copy.

The package is named for the role: @openhint/hintbook-marketing-manager.

No technical setup required from you

Ask your AI assistant to set it up

You need a folder with your documents and an AI assistant that can run commands in it: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or OpenCode. Which assistants can do this?

What will happen
The assistant creates hint.yml, installs the vocabulary, and tells itself how to use it. Nothing in your documents changes.
What you do next
Write rules in plain Markdown beside your documents. Your assistant reads the ones that apply before it works.
If you have no such assistant
Send this page to a technical colleague, or use the manual steps.

Your boundaries stay visible

What it will never do

  • State an unsubstantiated product or performance claim.
  • Use banned terms or disparage a competitor.
  • Apply one brand's voice, assets or claims to another brand.

Important boundary

HINT keeps brand and claim rules visible; marketing, legal and regulatory reviewers retain approval responsibility.

FAQ

Questions marketing managers ask

Does this make copy less creative?

No. It defines the brand and compliance boundaries; the assistant can explore freely inside them.

Can an agency manage multiple brands?

Yes. Shared agency rules live above client folders, while each brand's voice and assets remain isolated.

Does it approve regulated claims?

No. It identifies the declared disclaimer or review requirement and flags missing proof for the authorized reviewer.

For your technical colleague

The vocabulary

A hintbook is a vocabulary for your profession—installed, not written by you.

brand
Defines positioning and the identity whose rules apply.
audience
Names a declared segment for the message.
tagline
Preserves approved brand language verbatim.
campaign
Defines a campaign's goal, audience and period.
message
States a campaign message for a named audience.
claim
States a marketing assertion that requires support or review.
proof
Identifies evidence that substantiates a claim.
channel
Sets format, length and disclaimer constraints.

Manual setup

Bootstrap is read-only: it prints instructions for the assistant. The assistant performs the installation.

npm install -g @openhint/cli
hint config
hint add @openhint/hintbook-marketing-manager
hint apply
hint verify clients/acme/campaigns/spring-launch.md
hint emit clients/acme/campaigns/spring-launch.md
