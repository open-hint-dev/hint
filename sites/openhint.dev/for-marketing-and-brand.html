FAQ

Questions marketing & brand ask

Does HINT reduce creativity?

No. It supplies borders; creative choices remain open inside them.

Can an agency keep several brands?

Yes. Scope each client or brand to its own folder.

Does it approve legal claims?

No. It flags them for the declared reviewer.

Can it invent proof?

No. Proof and sources must be declared.
