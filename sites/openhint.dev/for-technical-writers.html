FAQ

Questions technical writers ask

Does it work with Docusaurus or MkDocs?

Yes. The output is plain Markdown.

Does it replace Vale?

No. Vale lints prose; HINT governs the intent and structure behind it.

Can it invent CLI flags?

No. Product facts must come from read or source blocks.

Is page structure deterministic?

Yes. The markdown emitter is model-free.
