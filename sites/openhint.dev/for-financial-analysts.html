FAQ

Questions financial analysts ask

Does it replace Excel?

No. HINT governs the model specification; the spreadsheet remains yours.

Is this investment advice?

No, explicitly.

Does HINT calculate the model?

It documents formulas and checks; your calculation tool executes them.

Can it invent a growth rate?

No. Inputs require a source and date.
